## 1. Purpose of This Document

This document defines the long-term product vision, mission, positioning, guiding principles and strategic direction of Niyuvia.

It explains why the product exists, whom it serves, what value it intends to deliver and how its capabilities should evolve.

The Product Requirements Document defines what the initial product must do. This document explains the broader direction behind those requirements.

## 2. Product Vision

To make recruitment more intelligent, transparent and accessible by combining practical workflow automation with responsible artificial intelligence.

Niyuvia aims to help growing businesses organize their hiring operations, understand candidate information and make better-informed recruitment decisions.

The long-term vision is to provide a unified recruitment workspace that supports the hiring lifecycle without removing human judgment from important employment decisions.

## 3. Product Mission

Our mission is to simplify recruitment operations for growing businesses through:

- Centralized candidate and job management.
- Structured resume information processing.
- Evidence-based candidate-job alignment.
- Organized recruitment workflows.
- Interview coordination and feedback management.
- Actionable recruitment analytics.
- Responsible AI assistance.

Niyuvia should make recruitment easier to manage without making the process unnecessarily complicated.

## 4. The Problem We Want to Solve

Many small and growing businesses rely on a combination of spreadsheets, email, messaging applications and disconnected recruitment tools.

This can lead to:

1. Scattered candidate information.
2. Repetitive manual resume review.
3. Inconsistent candidate comparisons.
4. Poor visibility into recruitment progress.
5. Disorganized interview feedback.
6. Limited recruitment performance insights.
7. Administrative overhead for small HR teams.

Niyuvia aims to bring these activities into one structured system.

These are initial product hypotheses. Customer interviews and real-world testing will be required to validate their frequency and business impact.

## 5. Target Audience

### 5.1 Primary Audience

Indian startups and SMBs that need a practical way to organize recruitment.

Initial target characteristics:

- Approximately 5–300 employees.
- Small HR or recruitment teams.
- Multiple active job openings.
- Limited internal recruitment infrastructure.
- Need for organized candidate records.
- Interest in practical AI-assisted workflows.

This segment is a starting hypothesis rather than a validated customer profile.

### 5.2 Primary Users

**Recruiters**

Need to manage job openings, review candidates, organize applications and coordinate interviews.

**Hiring Managers**

Need to review candidate information, assess relevant experience and provide interview feedback.

**Organization Administrators**

Need to manage users, permissions and organization-level settings.

**Candidates**

Need a clear and respectful application experience and appropriate handling of their personal information.

## 6. Product Positioning

Niyuvia is an employer-focused AI-assisted Applicant Tracking System.

It combines recruitment workflow management with structured candidate information processing and explainable candidate-job alignment.

### What Niyuvia Is

- A recruitment management platform.
- An applicant tracking system.
- An AI-assisted resume processing tool.
- An evidence-based candidate evaluation support system.
- A recruitment workflow and analytics workspace.

### What Niyuvia Is Not

- A complete HRMS.
- A payroll platform.
- An autonomous hiring decision system.
- A job-seeker productivity application.
- A general-purpose chatbot.
- A replacement for recruiters or hiring managers.

## 7. Core Value Proposition

**For recruiters:**

Reduce administrative effort and organize recruitment activities in one place.

**For hiring managers:**

Review structured candidate information and understand the evidence behind candidate assessments.

**For organizations:**

Improve visibility into recruitment operations and establish consistent hiring workflows.

**For candidates:**

Support more organized application handling and responsible treatment of personal information.

## 8. Product Differentiation Strategy

Niyuvia will explore differentiation through the following capabilities.

### 8.1 India-Focused Recruitment Workflows

Support recruitment information and terminology commonly used in India.

Examples:

- INR compensation.
- CTC and expected compensation.
- Notice period.
- Joining availability.
- Indian educational qualifications.
- Indian city and location information.
- Remote, hybrid and office-based work arrangements.

Personal and compensation information must be collected only when necessary and handled with appropriate access controls.

### 8.2 Evidence-Based Candidate Matching

Niyuvia should explain why a candidate appears aligned with a particular job.

Instead of showing only a numerical score, it should present:

- Relevant skills found.
- Relevant experience.
- Project evidence.
- Responsibility alignment.
- Missing or unavailable information.
- Areas requiring recruiter verification.

The score represents alignment with documented job requirements. It is not a probability of employment success or a measure of a person's overall worth.

### 8.3 Unified Recruitment Management

Bring important recruitment activities together:

- Job management.
- Candidate records.
- Resume processing.
- Applications.
- Recruitment stages.
- Interview coordination.
- Recruitment analytics.

### 8.4 Practical Automation

Prioritize automation that reduces repetitive administrative work.

Examples:

- Resume information extraction.
- Candidate record creation assistance.
- Application organization.
- Interview reminders.
- Recruitment activity summaries.

Automation should remain predictable, reviewable and permission-controlled.

## 9. Responsible AI Philosophy

Niyuvia will use AI as an assistant to human recruitment professionals.

Our principles:

1. Human hiring decisions remain mandatory.
2. Candidate assessments must be explainable.
3. AI-generated information must be reviewable.
4. Missing information must not automatically become negative evidence.
5. Protected characteristics must not be used for candidate scoring.
6. Candidate information must be handled responsibly.
7. AI outputs must be validated before being trusted.
8. AI should support recruiter productivity, not encourage blind reliance on automated scores.

Niyuvia must never present an AI-generated assessment as an objective guarantee of candidate suitability.

## 10. Product Design Principles

### Simplicity First

The interface should be understandable to recruiters who are not technical users.

### Evidence Over Guesswork

Candidate assessments should be grounded in available information.

### Human Control

Important recruitment decisions must remain under authorized human control.

### Privacy by Design

Personal information should be protected throughout collection, processing, storage and deletion.

### Maintainable Engineering

The system should be organized into clear modules with documented responsibilities.

### Cost-Conscious Development

The initial product should be developed using free and locally runnable tools wherever practical.

### Incremental Delivery

Features should be introduced in manageable stages and validated before expanding scope.

## 11. Product Strategy

Niyuvia will evolve through three broad stages.

### Stage 1 — Portfolio MVP

**Primary objective:** Build a working, demonstrable recruitment platform.

Focus:

- Job management.
- Candidate management.
- Resume upload and extraction.
- Structured candidate profiles.
- Candidate-job matching.
- Recruitment pipeline.
- Basic interview management.
- Dashboard analytics.

Success means demonstrating a complete recruitment workflow with reliable core functionality.

### Stage 2 — Product Validation

**Primary objective:** Determine whether real recruitment teams find the product useful.

Potential activities:

- Interview recruiters and hiring managers.
- Collect workflow feedback.
- Test usability.
- Evaluate resume extraction accuracy.
- Evaluate candidate matching quality.
- Identify frequently used features.
- Validate willingness to adopt or pay.

Commercial decisions should follow evidence rather than assumptions.

### Stage 3 — Commercial Production

**Primary objective:** Develop a secure, reliable and commercially sustainable SaaS product.

Potential investments:

- Managed infrastructure.
- Production AI integrations.
- Secure object storage.
- Monitoring and observability.
- Multi-tenant hardening.
- Data backup and recovery.
- Compliance and legal review.
- Billing and customer support.

This stage is not part of the zero-budget MVP commitment.

## 12. Product Success Philosophy

Niyuvia should not measure success only by the number of AI features implemented.

Product success should be evaluated through:

- Recruiter time saved on repetitive administration.
- Accuracy of extracted candidate information.
- Quality and traceability of candidate-job alignment.
- Recruitment workflow completion.
- Ease of use.
- User satisfaction.
- Reliability.
- Actual customer adoption.

Any quantitative claims about improvements must be supported by measured results.

## 13. Long-Term Product Direction

Subject to validation, Niyuvia may evolve into a broader recruitment intelligence platform.

Possible future capabilities include:

- Public career pages.
- Candidate application portals.
- Authorized email and messaging integrations.
- Advanced interview management.
- Interview question assistance.
- Recruitment automation.
- Advanced reporting.
- Organization-level configuration.
- Subscription and usage management.
- Integrations with external recruitment systems.

These are strategic possibilities, not approved MVP requirements.

## 14. Strategic Boundaries

To protect product focus, Niyuvia will avoid:

- Expanding into payroll or employee management prematurely.
- Building complex AI agents before core workflows work reliably.
- Adding integrations without a validated user need.
- Introducing microservices without architectural justification.
- Making paid services mandatory for local development.
- Claiming AI accuracy without evaluation.
- Claiming legal or security compliance without verification.

## 15. Product Principles for Future Decisions

When evaluating a proposed feature, ask:

1. Does it solve a real recruitment problem?
2. Does it support the needs of our target users?
3. Does it improve an existing workflow?
4. Can it be explained and maintained?
5. Does it respect candidate privacy?
6. Does it preserve human control?
7. Can we validate its usefulness?
8. Is it appropriate for the current development stage?

If a feature does not support the product direction, it should not be added merely because it is technically interesting.

## 16. Vision Statement

Niyuvia aims to make recruitment operations more organized, transparent and intelligent.

By combining structured recruitment workflows, practical automation and evidence-based AI assistance, the platform will help growing businesses manage hiring with greater clarity and confidence.

**Intelligent Hiring. Better Decisions.**
